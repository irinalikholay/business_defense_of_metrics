## Business Claim 

The business observes that Average Order Value (AOV) has increased and concludes that customers are spending more per order.

## Why This Claim Is Risky 

An increase in Average Order Value does not necessarily mean that most customers are spending more.
AOV can be heavily influenced by a small number of high-value orders, which may distort the overall picture of customer behavior.

## Business Risk 

If the company relies solely on AOV:
 - pricing decisions may be incorrect
 - marketing performance may be misinterpreted
 - customer value may be overestimated

 This can lead to poor strategic and operational decision.

 ## Distribution Reality Chech

 To validate whether AOV reflects typical customer behavior, we analyzed the distribution of order values.

 Key observations:
  - The median order value is significantly lower than the mean (AOV)
  - 75 % of orders are below a relatively low threshold
  - Only a small fraction of orders reach very high values

This indicates a highly right-skewed distribution , where most customers spend far less then the reported average.
